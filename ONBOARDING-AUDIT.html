✓Ran the whole thing on a synthetic warehouse sketch. The AI import is genuinely strong — it read 8 racks, the loading dock, the zone, and even the grid scale at 0.95 confidence, needing no review. Publish then built a 47-node routing graph and flipped the site to racked. A real receipt produced a bin recommendation, and accepting it moved the stock into that bin (ledger-verified).
But the import isn't publish-ready on its own — three gaps sit between "imported" and "operating": · The extracted walkways don't connect the dock to the racks, so the publish gate correctly blocks until a human draws the connecting aisles. Good that the gate has teeth — but the AI should get closer. · The dock is drawn on the perimeter wall, which blocks the route graph until the wall is opened at the dock. · Imported racks default to 10-slot pallet bins, and putaway won't split a line across bins — so a 48-unit receipt returns "no eligible bin" with no partial-placement option.

Fixed & deployed this session

2 blockers

Creating a product silently failed every time

catalog setup Fixed · live

The single most important onboarding action — adding a product — could not succeed through the UI. The form never collected a SKU or carton size and sent an empty image field the server rejects, and the dialog closed before the error surfaced, so it looked like it worked.

A second, independent blocker sat behind it: the form's default category Plant-Based wasn't in the server's allow-list (added to the app for a demo but never to the create endpoint), so even a corrected form was rejected.

FixAdded SKU + carton fields, real inline errors, keep-open-on-failure, and empty-image → null; synced the category list into mutate-product. Verified by creating a product live.
Commitsccf1e71 + mutate-product redeploy · 25 new tests

No way to adjust stock (shrinkage, damage, stocktake)

inventory Fixed · live

The ledger defined adjustment and stocktake movement types since day one, but nothing in the app could write them — a warehouse can't correct a miscount or write off breakage. Real customers hit this in week one.

FixNew inv_adjust_stock RPC + adjust-stock function (role-scoped, audited) + an Adjust action on the Stock screen. Delta and "set counted total" modes.
VerifiedShrinkage, stocktake variance, and clean rejections (below-reserved, empty reason) all confirmed live. Migration 00062, commit a497df1, 27 tests.

Open — high (blocks a real rollout)

3

Re-routing an order to another warehouse stalls it forever

order lifecycle Open · P1

When an operator processes an order and overrides the warehouse — say the nearest site is short and they push it to a second site — the stock reservation moves across correctly, but the original site is left with a leftover fulfilment record it can never fill. The order's overall status follows its least-finished site, so that empty leftover pins the order at "processed" permanently: the team picks and packs everything at the new site, but the order never advances to dispatched, and there's no way to clear it.

ReproOrder reserves at Warehouse One → Process → override to Warehouse Two → pick everything at Two → order is stuck at "processed" (verified in the ledger).
FixOn re-route, close out fulfilment rows for any warehouse that no longer holds part of the order.

Inviting a teammate is a dead end

users & access Open · P1

Two problems stack. The invite email pointed at localhost (auth had no production redirect configured) — fixed live during this audit. But even corrected, the invite link carries a sign-up token the app doesn't handle, so an invited warehouse user or customer lands on the login screen with no way to set a password.

ImpactYou cannot onboard the customer's own staff or buyers without a workaround. Test personas had to be created directly against the database.
FixHandle the type=invite link the same way password-recovery is handled, routing to a set-password screen.

No bulk import for a catalog or opening stock

onboarding cost Open · P1

A customer arrives with a stock list — often hundreds of lines. Today every product is entered one at a time, and opening balances require a separate goods-receipt per item (which also forces a supplier). There is no CSV or spreadsheet path.

ImpactOnboarding a real catalog is hours of manual entry — the biggest friction in the whole pitch. This audit had to script the catalog to proceed.
FixA CSV importer for products, and an opening-balance import that doesn't demand a supplier per line.

Putaway is only ever triggered from the receiving screen

warehouse intelligence Open · P2

Put-away recommendations are created only when stock is received through the UI. Stock that arrives any other way — a bulk load, an import, or a receipt where the advisory step quietly failed — lands at the warehouse root with no put-away task at all, and the Put-away queue shows "nothing to put away" even though stock is sitting unshelved.

FixCreate put-away tasks server-side on any receipt, not from the client.

AI-imported layout needs manual finishing before it can go live

warehouse intelligence Open · P2

The vision import places racks and the dock accurately, but doesn't connect the walkways into a route, and draws the dock on the wall — so publishing is blocked until an operator fixes both by hand. The gate working is good; the polish gap means "import your floor plan" isn't quite one-click. Separately, imported bins default to a fixed capacity and put-away won't split a large receipt across bins.

The "Add user" dialog can't be submitted on a short screen

ui robustness Open · P2

The user form doesn't scroll inside itself, so on a laptop-height window the Save and Cancel buttons sit below the fold — and there's no Escape or click-away to close. The dialog traps the whole screen until a page reload. (The product dialog scrolls correctly; this one just needs the same treatment.)

Receiving always says "Main Warehouse" and admins can't pick a destination

multi-warehouse Open · P2

The receiving screen is hard-coded to read "into the Main Warehouse" even for a warehouse user whose site is different, and an admin has no way to choose which warehouse to receive into. Stock does land in the right place (it follows the user's home site — verified), but the copy is misleading and admins are stuck with the default.

Picking is advisory, not directed — and the screen churns while it loads

fulfilment Open · P2

The warehouse-intelligence pick route is a suggestion the picker can ignore; picks aren't tied to the suggested bin, and the pick slip shows a different location than the route. Separately, the pick panel re-renders while computing the route, which drops fast clicks. The pick itself is correct — stock decremented accurately — it's the guidance and interaction that are loose.

No cancel, return, or short-ship path

order lifecycle Open · P2

Orders only move forward. There's no way to cancel an order and release its reserved stock, no returns/RMA flow, and a permanently short line blocks dispatch with no "ship what we have" option. Related: received stock is usable before it's put away (putaway is advisory), which is fine for speed but means the digital bin map can lag physical reality.
